CSV, XLSX & JSON Column Type Cleaner

Repair your data before you import it. This free online tool detects and fixes column type damage in CSV, XLSX and JSON files: corrupted dates, mangled phone numbers, broken numbers. It validates every column against your CRM's import rules, and everything runs entirely in your browser so your files never leave your computer.

What it fixes

  • Dates: repair coercion damage like lost leading zeros, ambiguous DD/MM vs MM/DD, and Excel-serialized numbers.
  • Phone numbers: validate and normalize to consistent formats for any country.
  • Numbers: restore stripped formatting, thousands separators, and currency values.
  • Text encoding: fix mojibake and broken characters from bad CSV encoding.

Why use it before a CRM import

Spreadsheets silently corrupt column types when opened, saved, or exported. A damaged date column or malformed phone number can fail a Salesforce, HubSpot, or Pipedrive import and cost hours of cleanup. This tool catches and repairs those problems before the import: entirely client-side, free, with no account and no upload.

How it works

  1. Drop in your CSV, XLSX, or JSON file. It is processed in your browser only.
  2. The tool analyzes every column and detects type damage automatically.
  3. Review suggested repairs, apply them, and download a clean file ready for import.

CSV, Excel XLSX, and JSON.
